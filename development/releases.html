The rules that keep this page honest

  • A build is a release only when recorded in a registry with its exact commit or tag, its evidence, and its status.
  • HELD is a first-class status, recorded in plain words — never omitted to make a project look further along.
  • No status changes without evidence on the exact commit; no release happens without the owner's explicit approval.
