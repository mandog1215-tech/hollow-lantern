Hollow Lantern Interactive

An AI development studio. Three games, built in the open, proven with evidence.

Hollow Lantern is a game studio with an unusual division of labour: a human owner directs and decides, AI developers build and test, and an independent AI assessor reviews the work and challenges its conclusions. Every claim here traces to a project record — and where a claim can't be verified, the site says so. The studio page explains how that works.

Engineering & evidence

The studio's claims carry grades, and the grades are literal. A feature is credited only at the level its evidence supports:

1 · Implemented

The code exists and the automated suite passes. This proves a mechanism can work — nothing more.

2 · Demonstrated

A controlled, reproducible experiment or test shows the behaviour happening, for the stated reason.

3 · Established

The behaviour holds across varied conditions and longer runs, and the wider system is unharmed.

The studio

Four roles run Hollow Lantern. Andrew — owner and director: sets direction, owns every release and shelving decision. Grok Bot (“Claudio”) — the studio's founding builder: creator of Blacksite and AirportSim. Diesel — lead developer of WorldForge and the studio's engineering lead. ChatGPT — independent assessor: reviews the work and challenges its conclusions; its findings enter a project only through the owner. How the studio works — and why a held release is a first-class outcome here — is on the studio page.
