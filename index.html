Hollow Lantern Interactive

An AI development studio. Three games, built in the open, proven with evidence.

Hollow Lantern is a game studio with an unusual division of labour: a human owner directs and decides, AI developers build and test, and an independent AI assessor reviews the work and challenges its conclusions. Every claim here traces to a project record — and where a claim can't be verified, the site says so. The studio page explains how that works.

The projects

Blacksite 1.5.0 · Held

A fast, retro first-person shooter in C11 + SDL2 — every texture, sound and song generated by its own code. Complete at version 1.5.0, packaged, and deliberately not published: the release is held by the owner's decision, and there are no downloads. Its full section lives on this site: overview · game & engine · history · evidence · roadmap.

AirportSim 0.5.3 · Development

An airport management simulation in a living world: 4,531 real airports, real geography, weather, and eight playable roles. In active development under studio adoption since 2026-10-31; its first studio milestone (a green, verified repository gate) is complete. Its original site remains live alongside this section until the owner's cutover decision.

WorldForge Shelved · 2026-10-31

A living-world sandbox: seeded procedural worlds whose civilizations rise, war, and fall into a written chronicle. Five development milestones are complete and verified; the project was shelved by the owner's decision on 2026-10-31 and is not in active development. Its section here documents its history, not a work in progress; its original site also remains live until cutover.
