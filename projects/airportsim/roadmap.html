WP-03 — Development Mode

Five stages integrated the F3 diagnostic overlay, state identity, invariant status, delay explanation, trace inspection, snapshot capture/compare, and measured step cost. Non-interference was tested with trace enabled and disabled, including a planted-draw negative control. Snapshot hashing was measured at about 1.95 ms, so full identity refresh is on demand rather than placed in the normal frame path.

Qualification: the integrated full gate exited 2. Matched base/current comparisons reproduced the business-window and topbar-overlap failure signatures, supporting attribution as pre-existing defects; this does not turn the original gate into a pass. The integration/merge stack remains subject to ordered verification.

Completed on the development line — evidence recorded through 2026-11-02

WP-04 — Performance baselines

A measured step-cost model was recorded: fixed overhead, active-aircraft count, and simulated time step. The fit is useful as a baseline, not yet a license to optimize: the aircraft-count coefficient is weakly identified because long-run windows confound world age with aircraft count. Performance budgets are accepted for future comparisons; no unmeasured largest-hub performance claim is made.

The authorized merge stack is to be integrated in dependency order, with checks rerun after each merge and mandatory stop conditions preserved around the known business-window failure. Phase 4 · Final gate

Polish and release readiness

Performance evidence (including the largest hubs, unmeasured today), stability and usability, a proven release pipeline, Windows release verification, and packaging. A release itself additionally requires the project owner's explicit approval — no approval, no release. Current builds are development builds; none is published.

Rules this map follows

  • Every milestone gets its own acceptance criteria before work starts; a milestone is complete only when its criteria pass with evidence.
  • Criteria are never weakened to obtain a pass; a blocked gate is documented, not walked around.
  • Nothing that already works gets rebuilt; nothing new is stacked on a known weak point until the weak point is fixed.
  • No delivery dates are invented; the milestone order is not silently changed.
  • Statuses update at milestone closeouts, from the development record.
