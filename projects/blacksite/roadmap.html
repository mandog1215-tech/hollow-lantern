Current position

A complete, held game with a proper home

Blacksite's development is cancelled, by the owner's decision of 2026-11-02: the game is finished to 1.5.0, its records are in order, and no further development is planned. The release question below is separate and remains the owner's to direct. The story of how it got here is on the history page.

The 1.5.0 release decision

Releasing 1.5.0, or retiring the hold, is a future owner decision; no date is attached to it, and nothing on this site should be read as a countdown. Until that decision exists, the version remains staged and held.
