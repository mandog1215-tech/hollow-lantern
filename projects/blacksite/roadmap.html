Current position

A complete, held game with a proper home

Blacksite's development is paused at a verified state: the game is finished to 1.5.0, its records are in order, and its future — release, further episodes, or continued hold — is the owner's to direct. The story of how it got here is on the history page.

Owner decision · unscheduled

The 1.5.0 release decision

Releasing 1.5.0, or retiring the hold, is a future owner decision; no date is attached to it, and nothing on this site should be read as a countdown. Until that decision exists, the version remains staged and held.

Episode 2: the Ironclad board

The project's own design records name a second episode as the largest unbuilt piece of the game. It is recorded here as intent from those records — no work is scheduled, and this page will only change when a decision and evidence exist.
